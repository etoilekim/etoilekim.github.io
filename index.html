Representing, reconstructing,
and understanding the visual world.

I am a Ph.D. student at Georgia Tech, advised by Sara Fridovich-Keil in the VOILA Lab. My research brings together computational imaging, signal representations, and inverse problems.

I study how representations and priors shape what we can recover from imperfect measurements. My work spans neural fields and explicit grids, training-free geometric priors for 3D reconstruction, and physics-based tomography—with an emphasis on understanding when a reconstruction can be trusted.
